Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the SubZen: Subscription Manager app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Fifty Three Studio (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address) and approximate location derived from it
  • The features of the Application that you use, interaction timestamps, and diagnostics
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / Android ID) and interaction data for advertising and analytics
  • User account details (such as email address and user ID) when you sign in, and subscription / bill management data (names, payment amounts, billing cycles, and categories)

The Application does not gather precise GPS location of your mobile device.


Local Storage & Cloud Synchronization

The Application utilizes a local-first architecture. Your subscription, billing, and preference data is stored locally on your device using encrypted and secure local storage. If you choose to sign in to your account, your data is securely synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore to allow backup, multi-device access, and recovery.


Advertising and Advertising ID (AD_ID)

The Application displays non-intrusive banner advertisements and voluntary rewarded video advertisements that you may choose to watch to unlock premium customizations or features. The Application does not display unexpected interstitial ads.

Advertisements are served through the Unity LevelPlay mediation network (including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads). In order to serve, optimize, and measure advertisements, these ad partners may access and process certain device information, including your device's Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), approximate location (derived from IP address), device identifiers, performance metrics, and app interaction data. You can learn more about how Unity processes data by visiting the Unity Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete advertising ID).


User Consent & Privacy Options (GDPR / UMP)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Application integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to obtain and manage user consent in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). When you first launch the Application, you will be presented with a Google consent form outlining how your data is used for advertising. You can review, modify, or revoke your consent choices at any time directly within the Application by navigating to Settings > Privacy Options, or through your device settings.

Permissions

To provide its services, the Application may request access to:

  • Network Access & Advertising ID: To load advertisements via Unity LevelPlay and synchronize subscription data with cloud storage.
