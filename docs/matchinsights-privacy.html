Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Match Insights for PUBG app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Fifty Three Studio (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Device identifiers and interaction data for advertising and analytics
  • PUBG Game Data (Stats, Match IDs) associated with your provided PUBG nickname
  • A random anonymous user ID (Firebase Authentication), used to store the player reports and coach notes you submit and to enforce their daily limits

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The Application shows banner ads, an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break (for example when leaving a match replay), and rewarded ads that you choose to watch to unlock the AI Coach analysis or the extended match history. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation by the ironSource Ads and Unity Ads networks.

  • To show and measure these ads, LevelPlay and its ad networks collect data from your device: the advertising ID, your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the Application asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Data Processing and AI Features

The Application uses your PUBG match data to provide advanced analytics. The AI Coach feature processes match statistics through third-party AI services (Google Gemini API, with OpenRouter as a fallback) to generate personalized coaching insights. No personal identity information (PII) other than your game nickname is sent to these services.
