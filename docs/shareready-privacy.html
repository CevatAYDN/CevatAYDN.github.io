Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the ShareReady app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Fifty Three Studio (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address) and approximate location derived from it
  • The features of the Application that you use, interaction timestamps, and diagnostics
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / Android ID) and interaction data for advertising and analytics

Local Video Processing

The core functionality of the Application (video compression, trimming, and format conversion) is performed locally on your device. The Application does not upload your original or compressed videos to any servers for processing or storage.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The Application displays non-intrusive banner ads, occasional interstitial ads at natural transition points (such as when video compression finishes), and rewarded ads that you may choose to watch to unlock premium features (such as hardware acceleration or advanced presets). Advertisements are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation by the ironSource Ads and Unity Ads networks.

  • To serve and measure these ads, Unity LevelPlay and its mediated ad networks collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address (and approximate geographic location derived from it), diagnostic data, and ad interaction metrics.
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Application presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before any ad requests are made. Users can review, change, or withdraw their consent at any time via device settings (Google Settings > Ads) or the in-app privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete advertising ID).

Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies. Below are the links to the Privacy Policy of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services
  • Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads

Permissions

To provide its services, the Application requests access to:

  • Photo Library / Storage / Media: To select videos for compression and save the processed results back to your gallery.
  • Network Access & Advertising ID: To load advertisements via Unity LevelPlay and sync application configuration.
