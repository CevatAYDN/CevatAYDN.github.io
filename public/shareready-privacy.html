Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the ShareReady app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Fifty Three Studio (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include information such as:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address) and approximate location derived from it
  • The features of the Application that you use, interaction timestamps, and diagnostics
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device
  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / Android ID) and interaction data for advertising and analytics

The Application does not gather precise GPS location of your mobile device.


Local Video Processing

The core functionality of the Application (video compression, trimming, audio muting, and GIF conversion) is performed entirely locally on your device using on-device processing libraries. The Application does not upload, stream, transmit, or store your original or compressed videos on any remote or cloud servers.


Advertising and Advertising ID (AD_ID)

The Application displays non-intrusive banner ads, occasional interstitial ads at natural transition points (such as when video compression finishes), and rewarded ads that you may voluntarily choose to watch to unlock premium features (such as hardware acceleration). Advertisements are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation network (including ironSource Ads and Unity Ads).

In order to serve, optimize, and measure advertisements, these ad partners may access and process certain device information, including your device's Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), approximate location (derived from IP address), device identifiers, performance metrics, and app interaction data. You can learn more about how Unity processes data by visiting the Unity Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete advertising ID).


User Consent & Privacy Options (GDPR / UMP)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Application integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to obtain and manage user consent in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2). When you first launch the Application, you will be presented with a Google consent form outlining how your data is used for advertising. You can review, modify, or revoke your consent choices at any time directly through device settings (Google Settings > Ads) or the in-app privacy settings.
