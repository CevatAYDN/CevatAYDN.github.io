Privacy Policy: Formatron: Zombie Line

Effective date: 24 November 2026 (updated whenever this text changes) · App: com.fiftythreestudio.formatron, on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone and iPad (App Store) · Developer: Fifty Three Studio · Contact: fiftythreestudio@gmail.com

Formatron: Zombie Line is a single-player runner game. It shows optional rewarded ads and a full-screen ad between some runs. It has one optional purchase that removes the full-screen ad, and no analytics. The game is the same on both platforms; where they differ, this policy says so.

No account: the game has no account, no login and no servers of its own, and we never ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal detail. The ad service described below collects data of its own.

On your device: your progress (the line you reached, coins, Armory upgrades, card evolutions, missions, the commander you picked, run statistics), the seed of your next run and your answer to the ad privacy question. All of this is stored, encrypted, in the game's own folder on your device. On Android the game turns backup off, so this data is not included in device or Google backups. On iPhone and iPad the game's folder can be included in your iCloud or computer backup, like other apps' data; it is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it from the device.

Ads (Unity LevelPlay):

  • A rewarded ad plays only when you press a button that starts with "Watch an ad" ("Reklam izle" in Turkish): when your squad falls, once per line, to call reinforcements; after a won line, to triple its reward. Saying no costs nothing, and the whole game can be played without them.
  • A full-screen ad can appear after the end-of-line screen: at most once every three lines, only after your first three sessions, never in the first minute of a session and never within 30 seconds of another ad. The game shows no banners.
  • To show and measure these ads, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with (ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and, in current or upcoming versions, Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collect data from your device: a device identifier, your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. On Android the identifier is the advertising ID. On iPhone and iPad the game never asks for permission to track you (there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt), so Apple's advertising identifier is not available to the ad service; it uses an identifier Apple gives each developer's apps on your device. Unity and its ad networks use this data under their own policies: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy Google AdMob uses it under Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • No data is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Newer versions limit ads to content suitable for all ages (G).

Purchase (Google Play or App Store): "Remove forced ads" ("Zorunlu reklamları kaldır" in Turkish) is a one-time purchase made through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone and iPad; the store processes the payment. It stops the full-screen ad between lines; the rewarded ads stay available, always optional. The game only learns that the purchase is complete; it never sees your payment details. The store tells the game which purchases your account owns, and Settings > Restore purchases asks it again.

  • The purchase runs through Unity's In-App Purchasing library, and that library, not the game, reports each purchase attempt and its result to Unity: the store's purchase record, the product and the price shown, your device model, system version and language, the app's name and version, and a random session ID. We cannot switch this off. Unity handles it under its own policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, before any ad is requested the game asks whether the ad service may personalise ads. Depending on your version of the game, the question is asked either by the game's own consent card (a Yes or No choice) or by Google's consent form (offered by Google's User Messaging Platform, a consent tool certified by the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) with the choices "Consent", "Do not consent" and "Manage options". Your choice stays on your phone, is passed to the ad service and its networks, and in both cases you can change it later in Settings > Privacy options. On Android the game reads your SIM card's country and your phone's region setting only to decide whether to ask; nothing is sent. In versions that use Google's consent form, people in US states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of their data from the same privacy options entry; that choice is also passed to the other ad networks in LevelPlay. More on how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

No analytics: the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting service; Unity's analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are switched off. Unity, the engine the game is built with, may send Unity basic technical information about the device (model, system version, graphics hardware) under the policy above. We do not sell your personal information.

Children: the game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your data: everything the game itself keeps is on your device; uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. To ask Unity what it holds about your device identifier, use the contact in its policy above; for any other question, write to us.
